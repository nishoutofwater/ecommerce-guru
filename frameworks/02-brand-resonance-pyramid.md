# Brand Resonance Pyramid (CBBE Model)

**Origin:** Kevin Lane Keller, Customer-Based Brand Equity model (1993, expanded 2001).

## Core idea
Brand strength is built bottom-up in four stages. Each stage answers a customer question. You cannot skip levels.

| Level | Building block | Customer question | E-com expression |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Identity | **Salience** | "Who are you?" | Category association, branded search volume, recall in the moment of need |
| 2. Meaning | **Performance** (rational) and **Imagery** (emotional) | "What are you?" | Product quality, delivery reliability, price/value; aesthetic, personality, who uses it |
| 3. Response | **Judgements** (head) and **Feelings** (heart) | "What do I think and feel about you?" | Reviews, quality perception, credibility; warmth, excitement, security, social approval |
| 4. Relationship | **Resonance** | "What about you and me?" | Repeat purchase, loyalty programme engagement, community, advocacy, UGC |

The left side (performance, judgements) is the rational route. The right side (imagery, feelings) is the emotional route. Strong brands build both.

## When to use
- Brand positioning for a new store or relaunch
- Diagnosing low repeat purchase or weak word of mouth
- Planning content pillars across the funnel
- Deciding where brand investment goes vs performance marketing

## Diagnostic questions
1. Salience: do people search for the brand name? What share of traffic is branded?
2. Performance: what do 1 to 3 star reviews complain about? (Fix before spending on imagery.)
3. Imagery: can a customer describe the brand personality in three words? Do they match yours?
4. Judgements and feelings: what words recur in reviews and social mentions?
5. Resonance: repeat purchase rate, referral rate, community participation.

## E-com application
- Resonance metrics map to retention: repeat rate, time between orders, LTV, referral share.
- Performance problems (late delivery, damaged goods) cap every level above. Ops is brand.
- Use the pyramid to audit content: most stores over-index on product imagery and under-invest in salience and resonance.

## Common misuse
- Jumping to community and loyalty programmes (resonance) before performance is consistent.
- Measuring only awareness and calling it brand.

## Pairs well with
STP (meaning for which segment?), 4Ps (performance and price shape meaning), Pygmalion (resonance through identity), RACE Engage stage, Racecar Lubricants (brand awareness).
