# RACE Framework

**Origin:** Dave Chaffey, Smart Insights. Digital marketing planning model. Often shown as Plan, Reach, Act, Convert, Engage (with Plan as a foundation stage).

## Core idea
Plan digital marketing across the full customer lifecycle, with KPIs per stage.

| Stage | Goal | E-com activities | Example KPIs |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Plan** | Strategy, goals, measurement | STP, positioning, budget, analytics setup | Targets agreed, tracking verified |
| **Reach** | Build awareness, drive visits | SEO, paid, social, PR, influencers, affiliates | Sessions, new users, reach, branded search |
| **Act** | Encourage interaction and lead capture | Content, quizzes, product discovery, email/SMS sign-up | Engagement rate, pages per session, sign-up rate, add-to-cart rate |
| **Convert** | Turn interest into sales | PDP optimisation, checkout, offers, retargeting, abandonment flows | Conversion rate, AOV, revenue, CAC |
| **Engage** | Build loyalty and advocacy | Post-purchase flows, loyalty, reviews, community, referrals | Repeat rate, LTV, NPS, review rate, referral share |

## When to use
- Building a full-funnel digital marketing plan
- Auditing where the funnel leaks
- Setting KPIs per channel and stage

## Diagnostic questions
1. Which stage has the worst ratio vs benchmark or vs last period?
2. Do we have tracking for each stage?
3. Is budget skewed to Reach while Convert or Engage leaks?
4. Who owns each stage?

## Common misuse
- Treating it as linear. Customers loop (Engage feeds Reach through referrals and reviews).
- Setting KPIs without baselines.

## How RACE and Racecar fit together
RACE is the **funnel map** (where the customer is). Racecar is the **growth mechanics** (what powers growth). Use RACE to find the leak and Racecar to choose the growth investment.

## Pairs well with
Racecar, TPB (Convert), Rule of 7 (Reach to Convert sequencing), Brand Resonance (Engage), Eisenhower (prioritise fixes).
