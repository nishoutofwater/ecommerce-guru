# Zeigarnik Effect

**Origin:** Bluma Zeigarnik (1927). Observation that unfinished tasks are recalled better than finished ones.

**Evidence note:** The original memory finding has had mixed replication. The related, better-supported idea is that open loops and visible progress create a pull toward completion (see also goal-gradient effect, Kivetz et al. 2006). Treat this as a useful design prompt, not a guaranteed lift. Test it.

## Core idea
People feel tension about incomplete tasks and are motivated to close them. In e-com, an unfinished cart, quiz, profile or reward is an open loop.

## E-com levers
| Lever | Example |
|---|---|
| Progress indicators | Checkout steps "2 of 3", profile completion bars |
| Saved state | Persistent carts across devices, "continue where you left off" |
| Abandonment recovery | Cart and browse abandonment email/SMS sequences |
| Quizzes and configurators | Product finder that shows partial results and asks to finish |
| Loyalty progress | "You're 1 order away from Gold", stamp cards, free shipping threshold bars |
| Content cliffhangers | Email series and story-led content that continues next send |
| Onboarding checklists | For subscriptions: "3 of 5 setup steps done" |

## When to use
- Reducing checkout or form drop-off
- Designing abandonment flows
- Boosting quiz completion and loyalty engagement
- Free shipping threshold design

## Diagnostic questions
1. Where in the funnel do users leave a task half-done?
2. Do we show progress, and do we remind them it's unfinished?
3. Is the remaining effort visibly small?
4. Does the reminder add value (saved item, low stock, answer to an objection) or only nag?

## Common misuse
- Fake progress or fake urgency. Erodes trust and may breach consumer rules on dark patterns.
- Over-messaging abandoners. Cap frequency and suppress after purchase.
- Long checkouts with progress bars. A bar does not fix too many steps.

## Pairs well with
TPB (Perceived control: show remaining effort is small), Rule of 7 (reminder cadence), Decoy (threshold offers), RACE Convert, Racecar Lubricants (conversion, retention).
