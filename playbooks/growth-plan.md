# Playbook: Growth Plan

**Use for:** "how do we grow", channel strategy, quarterly growth planning, stalled growth.
**Frameworks:** RACE (where the funnel leaks) + Racecar (what to invest in) + Rule of 7 (sequencing) → Eisenhower.

## Inputs to confirm
- Current metrics per RACE stage (traffic, sign-up rate, conversion rate, AOV, repeat rate, LTV, CAC). Ask for these; do not estimate.
- Channel mix and spend
- Category ad and payment restrictions
- Team capacity

## Steps

### 1. RACE diagnosis
For each stage (Plan, Reach, Act, Convert, Engage):
- Current KPI and trend
- Is tracking reliable?
- Biggest leak

### 2. Racecar diagnosis
- What is the growth engine today? Is it working, plateauing, or absent?
- Fuel check: content, customers, capital.
- Lubricant gaps: conversion, retention, awareness, activation, price.
- Is it time for a second engine or a mid-stage accelerant?

### 3. Choose levers
Match the leak to the lever:
| Leak | Likely Racecar lever |
|---|---|
| Low Reach | Kickstarts (early) or engine investment (SEO content, paid, referrals) |
| Low Act / Convert | Lubricants: conversion (see `conversion-audit.md`) |
| Low Engage | Lubricants: retention (see `lifecycle-and-retention.md`) |
| CAC rising | Second engine (SEO, virality, affiliates) |
| Plateau at scale | Mid-stage accelerants: channel partners, new geography, new category |

### 4. Sequence with Rule of 7
Map touchpoints from first exposure to purchase for the primary segment. Check variety and frequency caps.

### 5. Eisenhower
Prioritise, with a measurement plan per action (metric, baseline, test design).
