# Playbook: Pricing and Offers

**Use for:** price architecture, bundles, subscriptions, AOV, promotions.
**Frameworks:** 4Ps (Price) → Decoy → TPB → Zeigarnik → Eisenhower.

## Inputs to confirm
- Unit costs and margin (ask; never assume)
- Current prices, AOV, top SKUs
- Competitor price points (research if needed and cite)
- Goal: AOV, margin, subscription share, conversion

## Steps

### 1. Reference price
What does the segment compare against? Competitors, per-unit cost, alternatives.

### 2. Architecture
- Entry, core and premium options
- Bundles (1, 2, 3 pack or kits)
- Subscription vs one-off
- Identify the **target option** for each set

### 3. Decoy design
Add or adjust an option that is dominated by the target. Keep prices and savings real.

### 4. TPB check
- Attitude: is value obvious (per-unit price, savings shown)?
- Control: is cancellation of subscriptions easy and stated? (Required in many markets.)

### 5. Zeigarnik
Free shipping threshold bar, "add X to unlock".

### 6. Promotion policy
Discount frequency and depth. Avoid training customers to wait. Check local rules on reference pricing.

### 7. Test plan and Eisenhower
Define tests (metric: conversion, AOV and margin together).
