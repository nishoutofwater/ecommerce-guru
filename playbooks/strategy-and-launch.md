# Playbook: Strategy and Launch

**Use for:** new store, new brand, new product line, relaunch, brand health check.
**Frameworks:** STP → 4Ps → Brand Resonance → Racecar (Kickstarts, Turbo boosts) → Eisenhower.

## Inputs to confirm before starting
- Category and products
- Markets and shipping zones
- Platform (Shopify, WooCommerce, custom, marketplace)
- Stage (pre-launch, early, scaling)
- Margin structure and budget (ask; do not assume)
- Channel restrictions (ads, payments) for the category
- Existing customer data, if any

## Steps

### 1. STP
- List 3 to 5 candidate segments with needs, behaviours and reachability.
- Score each: size, reachability on allowed channels, margin, competition, fit.
- Pick a primary and a secondary segment.
- Write the positioning statement (`templates/positioning-canvas.md`).

### 2. 4Ps
- Product: hero SKUs, bundles, packaging, guarantee.
- Price: architecture and reference price (link to `pricing-and-offers.md`).
- Place: own site vs marketplaces vs retail; fulfilment speed per market.
- Promotion: channels allowed and suited to the segment.
- Consistency check: do all four Ps support the positioning?

### 3. Brand Resonance
- Salience: what search terms and category cues should we own?
- Performance: what must be reliably excellent (quality, delivery, support)?
- Imagery: personality, visual identity, user imagery.
- Judgements and feelings we want in reviews.
- Resonance plan: what makes customers return and talk (link to `lifecycle-and-retention.md`).

### 4. Racecar launch plan
- Choose likely growth engine(s) given category, margin and restrictions.
- Pick 3 to 5 kickstarts to reach the first customers (where does the primary segment gather?).
- Plan 1 to 2 turbo boosts around launch (PR, creator, drop).
- Confirm fuel: content pipeline, budget, seed customers.

### 5. Eisenhower
Sort every action. Output as a backlog with owners where known.

## Output
`templates/growth-plan.md` with STP and positioning at the top.
